# Frontier Command Centre: Indicative costs

**These figures are indicative only.** They are worked out from real installation activity and today's model prices. Your costs depend on how much your people use Claude, which features you switch on and which models you choose in Admin.

This covers only Command Centre's own use of Claude, such as grading work, matching sessions to use cases and writing briefings. Your people's own Claude Code, Claude Cowork and Claude Chat use is paid through your Claude plan, and hosting is separate.

## Claude API cost per month

| Use | Approximate cost |
|---|---|
| Trying it locally with sample data | $5 once, then a few cents a month |
| 100 people | $20 to $30 |
| 1,000 people | $200 to $300 |
| 5,000 people | $1,000 to $1,500 |

## What drives it

- **Matching sessions to use cases** and **suggesting department processes** use Sonnet, and make up about two-thirds of the cost.
- **Grading, the dashboard and personal briefings, descriptions and time estimates** use Haiku. On the newest Haiku they cost almost nothing.
- **Organisation and department briefings** use Sonnet by default, for accuracy with figures.
- More active people, more sessions and more use cases mean more calls.

## How to lower it

- Choose the newest Haiku for the grading and Core AI models, under Admin → Work Pulse, Developer Pulse and System.
- Several features use Sonnet by default. A cheaper model, chosen in Admin, lowers the cost but may change the quality of the results, so check a sample after switching.
- Switch off features you do not use. [AI data flow](ai-data-flow.md) lists each one and its switch.
